How Multi-Stage Incident Correlation & Prioritization Scoring Work

NetGuard correlates alerts within a 300-second window using Union-Find across two graph linking rules: (1) Same-Source Grouping (multiple alerts originating from the same Source IP) and (2) Victim-to-Source Pivot (a target of an earlier alert subsequently initiates outbound alerts). Incident scores (0–100) combine strongest alert severity (up to 35 pts), stage diversity (up to 30 pts), chronological kill-chain order (up to 15 pts), and ML/outbound corroboration (up to 20 pts). Scores are prioritization heuristics, not statistical probabilities.
