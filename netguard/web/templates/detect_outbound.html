How Outbound Heuristics Work & Normal Browsing Considerations

Evaluates internal-to-external flows in 20s windows across three heuristic conditions: (1) High Volume (≥{{ "%.1f"|format(config.bytes_per_window / 1048576) }} MB egress), (2) Asymmetric Upload Ratio (≥{{ config.upload_ratio }}:1 outbound-to-inbound ratio when egress ≥200 KB), and (3) Unusual Port Transfer (≥{{ "%.0f"|format(config.unusual_port_bytes / 1024) }} KB on ports outside {{ config.allowed_ports | join(', ') }}).

Analyst Interpretation Note: Legitimate HTTPS uploads, cloud backups, or telemetry bursts on port 443 can produce high outbound-to-inbound ratios (`outbound_ratio`). Review the destination IP, port, and corroborating alerts before treating a single ratio indicator as malicious exfiltration.
